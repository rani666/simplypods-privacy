Simply Pods Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Simply Pods (“the app”) is a podcast player for Android published by Rani Rimon (“we”). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. It applies to the Simply Pods app distributed on Google Play.

In short: there's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We don't run a server and don't receive any of your data. Your library stays on your phone. When you search, your search words go directly to Apple's podcast search service, and the app then contacts the websites that host the shows you choose.

1. Information stored only on your device

The app stores these items in its private storage on your device:

  • the shows you subscribe to and their episode lists
  • playback position, played status, the “Up next” queue, the recently played list, and the episode that was playing when the app was closed
  • download status, and the audio files you download

We can't see or access this information.

Android backup. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may copy the app's database and settings (not the downloaded audio) to your Google account, so a new or restored phone can keep your library. Google runs this backup under its own terms. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

2. Information sent from your device

The app sends data off your device only to provide the features you use:

WhenSent toWhat is sent
You search for a podcastApple's iTunes Search service (itunes.apple.com)The words you type and the region you select
You open DiscoverApple's iTunes serviceA request for Apple's public list of popular podcasts in Israel. No personal information.
You subscribe to, refresh, stream or download a show (and every 6 hours for shows you subscribe to)The website that hosts that show's RSS feed, audio files and artworkA normal web request for that file

Like any internet request, these requests reveal your device's IP address to the server that receives them. Each request also includes a technical header (User-Agent) with the app name, the app version and a link to the app's project page (https://github.com/rani666/simplypods). It contains no name, account, advertising ID or device identifier.

Apple handles search requests under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. Podcast hosts are independent third parties. Their handling of requests is covered by their own policies, which we don't control.

All connections made by the app are encrypted (HTTPS).

3. What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising, advertising ID, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal information, and we don't receive it in the first place.
  • The app doesn't access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, SMS or call log.

5. Android Auto

If you use Android Auto, your car display shows your shows, downloads, queue and recent episodes from the app on your phone. The app doesn't send this information anywhere.

6. Retention and deletion

Everything the app stores stays on your device until you remove it:

  • Delete a download in Downloads or on the episode screen.
  • Remove an episode with Remove from library.
  • Unsubscribe from a show on its page. Its playback progress stays on your device so it's restored if you subscribe again.
  • To erase everything, go to Android Settings → Apps → Simply Pods → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Copies in Android backup follow your Google account's backup settings. Because we hold no data about you, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Children

Simply Pods isn't directed at children. It's intended for users aged 16 and over. Podcasts come from independent creators and can contain mature content. The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the “Last updated” date, and include the updated policy in the app.
